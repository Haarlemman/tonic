The University of Free Will

Tonic for the Bones
"Not the universe. The university."

This project gives an intimate look at the life and philosophy of David Enker, a Dutch-based writer and designer living with Parkinson’s disease.

Rather than viewing illness solely as a limitation, I frame it as a "tonic" that offers unexpected clarity and a deeper appreciation for the present moment.

Through a series of poignant short stories, I explore the complex intersections of trauma, adoption, and hereditary legacy.

The work reflects deeply on my Jewish ancestry and the "inherited fear" passed down through generations, alongside the daily physical and mental challenges of a neurological diagnosis.

Ultimately, this is a creative testimony to resilience—urging a shift from seeking control to embracing a journey of transcendence, where art and family become essential tools for navigating a body in constant transition.
