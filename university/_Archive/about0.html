System Vision

The University operates on a single axiom: Adversity is your teacher.

Life is a composition of surprises and twists. Within the rigid framework of existence, we find our agency. My vision is to map the architecture of this resilience—the 3 angles of truth: the light, the shadow, and the grit in between.

The Publication

Following Phantom Parents, this second work is a deep dive into the physical and psychological foundations we stand upon. It is a collision of worlds—graphic novel elements meet philosophical inquiry.

It explores how we rebuild ourselves when our physical foundation—the "bones"—is challenged. It merges the intellectual with the relatable, inspired by the daily wit of my son, Yaron, and the grounding presence of my wife, Ailish.

Paradox of the Tremor

In 2021, the diagnosis arrived: Parkinson's Disease.

"It is like breakdancing while trying to type a love letter. It demands a new kind of rhythm."

Awareness is visibility. The creative mind does not slow down; it simply recalibrates. At the University, we teach that the tremor is not a defect, but a different frequency of progress.
