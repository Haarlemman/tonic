The Stand-Up
Parkinsonian

By David Enker  |  Illus. by Yaron Enker

Riding the wave of Wednesday’s football success, Yaron wanted to go again. I’ve never been much of a football fan, but Parkinson’s demands I stay physically active, so I’ve leaned into it. I felt great after joining the boys, running after the ball, kicking it away. Almost symptom-free.

Thinking I could push through my “off” period by staying physical, I ran after the ball and made a move you’d expect from someone who’d booboo’ed the sport most of his life. It was a lesson in elementary physics: action > reaction.

My foot hit the ball at the wrong angle, slid over it, dragged my leg, followed by the rest of my slightly heavier-than-ever body, diving via a quantum loop and a black hole straight to the floor.

Yaron looked at me with an infinitely sad gaze frozen in time, yelling my nickname, like something painted by Edvard Munch.
