The University of Free Will

"Not the universe. The university."

My seven-year-old son once corrected me. He said life isn't a universe, but a university. He was right. It’s a strange kind of school where you show up unprepared and the lessons don’t make sense. This website is my attempt to navigate the curriculum.

Part of living with Parkinson’s—or just living—is about losing control. You don’t control your muscles, the timeline, or the chaos. But life gets a whole lot more interesting once you let go of the desire to control.

This platform is a "login" to that university. It offers a Tonic: tools designed to make you feel stronger rather than treating a specific problem.
