The University of Free Will

"Not the universe. The university." — Yaron

This project gives an intimate look at the life and philosophy of David Enker. Through a series of poignant short stories, I explore the complex intersections of trauma, adoption, and hereditary legacy.

Rather than viewing illness solely as a limitation, I frame it as a "tonic" that offers unexpected clarity. This campus is a creative testimony to resilience—urging a shift from seeking control to embracing the beauty of the present moment.

— Welcome to the Campus.
