The Star of Life

A Map of Meaning

"We are not born into a home; we are born into a task."

This is an interactive map of a life fractured by adversity, yet reconstructed through meaning. Struck by the parallels between my own life's history and the one by the most misunderstood people on Earth, I felt inspired to create and share this epic journey.

Each station contains three layers of insight: Personal (Memoir), Collective (Jewish History), and Cosmic (Philosophy).

Click the stations to advance the path and reconstruct the Star.
