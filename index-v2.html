WHAT IS THIS PLACE

In the beginning was darkness. Life, in its basic form, is void and meaningless. We come from dust and return to dust. What's left are the dry bones.

Somewhere in between — where you least expect it — is the light, the joy, the love. The tonic that feeds your soul.
