        How to Use This Framework

        For Personal Challenges:

        • • Identify which domain you naturally favor
        • • Explore approaches from neglected domains
        • • Look for integration opportunities
        • • Notice which domain might be most healing

        For Understanding Others:

        • • Recognize their dominant domain approach
        • • Appreciate different but valid perspectives
        • • Bridge communication gaps between domains
        • • Offer support in their preferred language
