1. What’s Wrong With Sitting Still?

Most creative tools assume you're seated, still, and typing. But our bodies are made to move—especially when we think, create, or feel. For people with Parkinson’s, this isn't just about comfort. It’s about access, expression, and freedom.

“What if the studio moved with us, instead of pinning us down?”

2. A Studio That Moves

The Standing Studio is voice-first, gesture-aware, and body-friendly. It’s powered by AI and designed for real human rhythms. Whether you're walking, stretching, or pacing, your tools listen and respond.

3. Setup Examples

  • Micro Studio: Just a headset, a mic, and smart software.
  • Home Creator Studio: Standing desk + webcam + smart assistant + projector or second screen.
  • Immersive Studio: Full-body motion capture, large projection wall, real-time feedback.

4. Why It Matters

  • 🧠 Supports neuroplasticity and Parkinson's-related movement
  • 💬 Encourages natural communication through voice and gesture
  • 🧍‍♂️ Reduces sedentarism and improves posture
  • 🔄 Boosts productivity by aligning with embodied flow
  • 🤝 Makes digital creation more inclusive
“This is about turning technology into a dance partner.”

5. Our Vision

We believe in a future where creativity moves with the body. Where neurodiverse people lead the way in reshaping human-computer interaction. Where tools don't just assist—they liberate.
