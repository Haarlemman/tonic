The Standing Studio

Reimagining creativity through movement. An inclusive, voice-first workspace empowering neurodiverse creators to think, move, and create freely.

What Is the Standing Studio?

The Standing Studio is a voice-activated, movement-oriented creative environment for artists, writers, and thinkers — especially those living with Parkinson's or other neurodiverse conditions. Instead of sitting at a desk, users stand, speak, and move while interacting with creative tools like Photoshop, After Effects, and writing software, all powered by intelligent voice and gesture interfaces.

Why the Standing Studio?

  • Reduces strain and supports healthy posture
  • Enhances accessibility and neurodiverse inclusion
  • Boosts creativity through embodied movement
  • Voice-first interface for hands-free productivity
  • Built with Parkinson’s challenges in mind
