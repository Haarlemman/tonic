Embodied Virtual Creativity

The Standing Studio is a modular, voice- and gesture-enabled workspace designed for people with Parkinson’s—and for anyone tired of being hunched over a screen. It transforms creative and digital tasks into a liberating experience using immersive and accessible technology. You can stand, move, speak, and create—without being tied to a desk.

Studio Setup

Micro Studio

A standing desk, voice assistant (e.g., Mac Dictation or Whisper), and basic motion or shortcut controls. Affordable, accessible, and ready to go.

Home Studio

Adds a large screen, headset or glasses, and custom software for a deeper, more interactive experience. Designed for remote creative and cognitive flow.

Immersive Studio

Full VR environment with AI tools, gesture tracking, and custom workflows for writing, designing, and thinking in space. It’s not a game—it’s a new way to work, think, and be.

Key Features

  • 🌐 Modular: Build up from simple to immersive.
  • 🗣️ Voice-First: Speak your text and commands.
  • 🕹️ Embodied: Move while you think and create.
  • 🎨 Creative: Use with writing, design, or learning tools.
  • 🧠 AI-Enhanced: Integrate with smart assistants, creative co-pilots, and spatial AI.
  • 💸 Affordable: Aimed at accessibility, with potential subsidies and partnerships.
